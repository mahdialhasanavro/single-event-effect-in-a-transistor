Schematic, not to scale. The ion deposits about 10.8 fC per µm for each MeV·cm²/mg of LET. The drain collects charge by drift inside the depletion region, by funneling (field extends deeper along the track), and by slower diffusion. The struck drain is the OFF NMOS of the inverter holding Q = 1; its current pulse discharges Q while the cross-coupled inverters fight to restore it. Qcrit is found by scaling this pulse until the cell just flips.
